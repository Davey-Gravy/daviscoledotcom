I think capitalism is working if you make one assumption; that we're living in hell.

I believe that capitalism is the best way to tap into a person's motivation and drive innovation.

*Innovation*. I'm starting to sound like a FAANG PM. Unfortunately, capitalism's biggest winners don't seem to embody capitalism, but socialism.

President Eisenhower explicitly called out the military-industrial complex (after supporting nuclear escalation), and we all know about Boeing, Lockheed, Raytheon, etc.

However, lobbies exist in every industry; food (Cargill, Tyson), oil & gas (Exxon, Chevron), transportation, tax filing. Seriously, it's quite depressing to see the state of things. 

Ideally our government would have teeth and regulate things that actually matter, like serving public interests rather than private interests. The individual has so much less power and capital than the corporation, so why do we keep stacking the deck against the individual?

People shit on capitalism, but I think their blame is misplaced on the economic system. I bet any economic system could work if tailored to the culture it's being implemented in.

So there you go, a cop-out non-answer.
