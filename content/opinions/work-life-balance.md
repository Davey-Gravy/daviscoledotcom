If you've read any of this blog you'll know where I stand on this.
I think it's impossible to live a fulfulling life without some form of work.

I've certainly taken this to the extreme, where I could experience a lot more life with doing very little work, but we create prisons for ourselves to cope with reality.

Knowing that I'd rather troubleshoot Azure DevOps Pipelines CI failures at 2am instead of going out and drinking makes it obvious who I am.

I find that this opinion has a pretty fine divide. Not too many exist in the middle of work-life balance.
