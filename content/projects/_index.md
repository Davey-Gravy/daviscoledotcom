I'm a mechanical engineering PhD student at WPI, working on simulations of multiphase flow. A lot of that work is software: setting up cases, running them on workstations and clusters, keeping track of what each run was for, and checking whether the answers are right.

Before grad school I worked in verification and testing, at DEKA and then on the Fluent testing team at Ansys.

What's here is the work I can show: simulation projects, the tools I've built around them, and the computers I run at home. Code is linked where it's public.
