nighthawK is a 3D modeling environment for data centers that runs in the browser, with an Electron wrapper for the desktop. You build a room from a library of equipment (server racks, CRAC units, in-row coolers, perforated floor tiles, blanking panels, cable trays), select and move pieces in the scene, and edit their properties in a side panel: a rack's power load and inlet temperature, a cooling unit's capacity, supply temperature and airflow, a tile's open area.

About 1,800 lines of JavaScript on three.js.
